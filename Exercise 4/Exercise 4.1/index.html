Energy Consumption in Australia

Household appliances are an important part of everyday life. Appliances such as televisions, refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners consume electricity when they are operating.

Understanding appliance energy consumption can help households identify opportunities to reduce electricity use and improve energy efficiency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Energy consumption refers to the amount of electricity used by an appliance or household over a period of time.

Energy-efficient appliances can use less electricity while providing the same or similar function. This can help reduce electricity consumption.

Households can reduce energy consumption by choosing efficient appliances, switching appliances off when they are not needed and avoiding unnecessary standby use.
