Televisions and Energy Use

Televisions are common household appliances in Australia. Their electricity consumption can vary depending on the screen size, display technology, brightness and how long the television is used each day.

Turning a television off when it is not being used can help reduce unnecessary electricity consumption.

Tips for Reducing Television Energy Use

  • Turn the television off when it is not being watched.
  • Reduce screen brightness when appropriate.
  • Avoid leaving the television on for long periods without use.
  • Consider energy efficiency when purchasing a new television.
